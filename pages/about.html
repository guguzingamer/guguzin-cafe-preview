AI use

So, my opinion about AI is: They are here to help you, not to make things for you. I also will never truly use AI assets in my projects outside of Placeholders.

Let's be honest, we are in the big 2026, who doesn't use AI, it's everywhere.

And, to be transparent about my projects, I will categorize them in four types:

None There is nothing AI generated in it.
Low Contains some modified code, but not enough to be considered significant.
Moderate AI was used to speed up the creation. Still not enough.
Considerable Has a significant amount of AI generated code.

I know that we are in the AI era, but I prefer being familiar with my own code.
